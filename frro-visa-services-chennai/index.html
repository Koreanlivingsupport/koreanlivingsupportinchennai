FRRO and visa services in Chennai for Korean expats

KLS handles FRRO registration in Chennai for Korean expats and their families, from the first registration after arrival to visa extension, residential permit renewal and the exit permit when you leave India. We prepare your documents, file on the e-FRRO portal and explain each step in Korean.

FRRO registration after arrival

Foreigners on long-term visas, such as employment or business visas for more than 180 days, must register with the FRRO soon after arrival. Your visa usually states the deadline. We collect your documents, fill the e-FRRO application, book any appointment and follow up until your Residential Permit is issued.

Family members on dependent visas are registered at the same time, so the whole family is covered in one process.

Visa extension and residential permit renewal

We track your permit expiry date and start the renewal well before it lapses. You get a clear list of documents in Korean, including the letters your employer needs to provide, and we handle the online filing.

Exit permit before leaving India

When your assignment ends, you may need an exit permit before you can leave. We prepare the application together with your employer so your departure date is not delayed.

PAN card and income tax for foreigners

  • PAN card: needed for your bank account, salary and many daily payments.
  • Income tax (ITR) filing: we coordinate your annual return with a qualified tax professional.
  • C-Form: the landlord's or hotel's report of your stay, arranged when you move in.

Frequently asked questions

Who needs to register with the FRRO in Chennai?

In general, foreigners on visas valid for more than 180 days, such as employment visas, and their dependants. Your visa conditions decide the exact rule; we check them for you.

Can you handle FRRO for all our company's Korean staff?

Yes. We manage registrations and renewals for whole teams and keep one expiry calendar for your HR team.

Do I need to visit the FRRO office?

Most services are online through e-FRRO. If a visit is requested, we prepare you and can accompany you.

Do you give legal advice on visas?

We handle the process and paperwork. For legal questions about visa eligibility, we work with qualified immigration lawyers.
