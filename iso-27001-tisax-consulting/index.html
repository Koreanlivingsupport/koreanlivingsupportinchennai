Frequently asked questions

How long does ISO 27001 certification take?

It depends on your size and starting point. A small or mid-size site often needs several months from gap assessment to audit.

Can reports be in Korean for HQ?

Yes. We provide summaries in Korean and full documentation in English.

Do you issue the certificate?

No. Certificates are issued by accredited certification bodies. We prepare you and support you through their audit.

Do you support companies outside Chennai?

Yes. Much of the work can be done remotely, with site visits as needed.
