Frequently asked questions

How long does it take to find a home in Chennai?

Most searches take one to three weeks, depending on area and budget.

Can you start before I arrive in India?

Yes. We can shortlist homes and run video viewings before you land.

What does a typical lease in Chennai include?

Usually an 11-month agreement, a security deposit and a notice period. We explain each term in Korean before you sign.

Do you help after the lease is signed?

Yes. Repairs, owner issues and renewal are part of our service.
